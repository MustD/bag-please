Epic 4 — Design Direction Exploration

The overall design direction is settled (the design/ prototype). This file explores the three open visual decisions: how lifecycle status appears on item rows, and what the list-complete state looks like.

Section A — Item Row Lifecycle Indicator

How does Mia know at a glance that an item is a one-timer or recurring? Three approaches — pick the one that reads most clearly.

Variant A
Trailing pill badge
Badge appears at the right of the row. "1×" for one-timers, "W" / "2W" / "M" for recurring. Icon-free — purely typographic.

Variant B
Inline sub-text indicator
Lifecycle info appears in the metadata line below the item name. "Gone after check-off" for one-timers; "↻ weekly" for recurring.

Variant C
Leading icon dot
A small colored dot left of the checkbox signals lifecycle type. Teal = recurring, soft red = one-timer. Tooltip/label only in item editor.

Section B — List Complete State

When the last item is checked, what does Today look like? Two approaches — one subtle, one more rewarding.

Variant B1
Silent fill
Progress bar turns green and fills. A quiet "All done" line replaces the item count. No banner. Calm.

Variant B2
Completion card
Progress bar turns green. A small card appears below the progress strip: "You're done · Ready to go." Dismissible. Disappears after 8s.
